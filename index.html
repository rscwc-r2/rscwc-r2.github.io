Statutory Frameworks

RSCWC Region II Flagship Programs

Translating national child welfare statutes into concrete regional programs across Survival, Development, Protection, and Participation.

Masterplan 2023 - 2028

Regional Plan of Action for Children (RPAC)

Multi-sectoral master plan unifying DSWD, DepEd, DOH CVCHD, DILG, and NEDA across all 5 provinces to eliminate stunting and enhance juvenile protection.

Governance Seal DILG-CWC Joint

Child-Friendly Local Governance Audit (CFLGA)

Annual audit assessing all 93 LGUs on child budget earmarks (1% IRA), daycare service quality, and functional Barangay Councils for Children.

Disaster Protocol RA 10821

Comprehensive Emergency Program for Children (CEPC)

Mandatory safeguarding protocol during river basin flooding and island typhoons, ensuring Child-Friendly Spaces (CFS) and minor registries.

Cyber Safety RA 11930

Anti-OSAEC & CSAEM Regional Taskforce

Coordinated action against Online Sexual Abuse and Exploitation of Children, aligning digital forensics with PNP RACU 2 and school campaigns.

Early Education 1,840+ CDCs

ECCD Center & Worker Accreditation

Standardization of Child Development Centers across Region II, guaranteeing continuous education for Daycare Workers and nutritious feeding.

Makabata 1383 & Bahay Pag-asa Network

RSCWC REGION II

The Regional Sub-Committee for the Welfare of Children (RSCWC Region II) is the regional arm of the Council for the Welfare of Children (CWC), mandated to protect, uphold, and monitor child rights across Cagayan, Isabela, Nueva Vizcaya, Quirino, and Batanes.

DSWD FO2, Carig Sur, Tuguegarao City
