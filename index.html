Annual audit in partnership with DILG Region II assessing all 93 LGUs on child budget earmarks (1% IRA), daycare service ratios, adolescent health, and BCPC institutional capacity.

Comprehensive Emergency Program for Children (CEPC)

Statutory protocol safeguarding minors during seasonal Cagayan River flooding and Batanes super-typhoons. Mandates Child-Friendly Spaces and anti-trafficking registries in shelters.

RSCWC REGION II

The Regional Sub-Committee for the Welfare of Children (RSCWC Region II) is the regional arm of the Council for the Welfare of Children (CWC), mandated to protect, uphold, and monitor the rights of every child across Cagayan, Isabela, Nueva Vizcaya, Quirino, and Batanes.
